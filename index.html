About YRC

Our Mission & Principles

The Youth Red Cross (YRC) at Easwari Engineering College operates under the Indian Red Cross Society (established 1920) and serves as an active platform for student-led humanitarian work.

Origin of the Movement

The roots of the Red Cross can be traced to the mid-19th century by the visionary Henry Dunant. The Indian Red Cross Society (IRCS) was formed in 1920, and the Youth Red Cross was introduced in India in 1968 to engage young minds in volunteer service.

At Easwari Engineering College, we carry forward this flame by nurturing social responsibility, health promotion, disaster responsiveness, and voluntary service among engineering students, bridging professional development with community action.

01. Promoting Health & Hygiene

Organizing health checkup camps, sanitation awareness, and hygiene outreach campaigns.

02. Voluntary Blood Donation

Conducting massive annual donation camps to support government hospital blood banks.

03. Disaster Relief & Response

Training in emergency preparedness, first aid operations, and resource mobilization.

04. Community Outreach

Supporting old age homes, child orphanage centers, and cancer hospital care institutes.

The Seven Fundamental Principles

H
Humanity

To prevent and alleviate human suffering.

I
Impartiality

No discrimination by nationality, race, or belief.

N
Neutrality

Never take sides in controversies or conflicts.

I
Independence

Maintain autonomy relative to national laws.

V
Voluntary

Not prompted in any manner by desire for gain.

U
Unity

Only one Red Cross society in any country.

U
Universality

All societies share equal status worldwide.

AK

"The Youth Red Cross stands as a pillar of selflessness, empowering our students to lead with empathy, action, and social responsibility. As Faculty In-Charge, it is a privilege to guide our dynamic team in saving lives, building disaster preparedness, and driving impactful community welfare across Easwari Engineering College."
