Event Summary

The Youth Red Cross of Easwari Engineering College (Autonomous) is thrilled to announce the successful completion of “The First Aid Awareness Session” held on 17th October 2025 at the MBA Seminar Hall, in celebration of First Aid Day.

The session was led by Pulavar Dr. R. Manikam, Secretary of the Indian Red Cross Society, Chengalpattu District, who shared valuable insights on emergency response, CPR, and life-saving techniques, emphasizing how quick action can save lives.

Our 123 enthusiastic students and Youth Red Cross Office Bearers actively participated, gaining practical knowledge, confidence, and the mindset to act responsibly during emergencies.
