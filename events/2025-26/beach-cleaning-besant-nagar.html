Beach Cleaning Event at Besant Nagar Beach

20th September 2025 • 6:00 AM – 9:00 AM
Besant Nagar Beach, Chennai
Tenure 2025-26

Event Summary

The Youth Red Cross (YRC) of Easwari Engineering College successfully organized a massive Beach Cleaning Event at Besant Nagar Beach on 20th September 2025.

The initiative witnessed the active participation of 287 dedicated student volunteers, who worked with great enthusiasm and commitment towards restoring the beauty of Chennai's coastline.

During the drive, volunteers collected and segregated 173 bags of waste, ranging from single-use plastic bottles and wrappers to non-biodegradable debris that poses a critical threat to marine life. Beyond cleaning the shore, the event raised public awareness regarding responsible waste management and marine conservation.
