Blood Donation Camp at Govt. Hospital, Valasaravakkam

17th September 2025 • 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM
Govt. Hospital, Valasaravakkam, Chennai
Tenure 2025-26

Event Summary

The Youth Red Cross (YRC) of Easwari Engineering College organized a high-impact Blood Donation Camp at the Government Hospital in Valasaravakkam on Wednesday, 17th September 2025.

With immense enthusiasm and compassion, our student volunteers stepped forward to donate 38 units of precious blood, providing vital medical support to emergency patients and healthcare facilities in the community.

Under the leadership of Event Convener Dr. R. Arunkumar, the drive highlighted the true spirit of Red Cross voluntary service and civic responsibility among young engineers.
